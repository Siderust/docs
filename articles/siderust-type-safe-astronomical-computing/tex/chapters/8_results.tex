Earth rotation angle and IAU 2006 Greenwich mean sidereal time agree with ERFA to below
$\RotationBound$ rad on the evaluated grid. Mean obliquity and the bias--precession--nutation

For the 13 civil UTC labels, including three inserted seconds, the native split-second
representation preserves agreement at a finer resolution than a single floating-point Julian date.

With the same DE440s kernel, maximum Earth heliocentric and Moon geocentric position residuals are
$\EarthSpkMax$ au and $\MoonSpkMax$ km. Coverage probes one second inside each boundary succeed,
while probes one day outside return errors. The recorded endpoint probes first convert
kernel-native TDB dates to TT and are then rejected by the runtime ephemeris path after its time
conversion and representation steps. They therefore concern converted TT queries, rather than
direct evaluation of the native TDB endpoints; the native evaluator uses closed coverage bounds.

\subsection{Integrated astronomical applications}

The Antares altitude series has maximum ERFA residual $\ViewingAltitudeResidual$ degrees, within
the $\AltitudeBound$-degree bound. Independently located threshold crossings differ by at most
$\ViewingEventResidual$ s, within the \ViewingEventTolerance{} s search tolerance. The interval,
culmination and sampled altitude curve are given in Section~\ref{sec:antares-example}.

For the Earth-from-Moon observation in Section~\ref{sec:lunar-example}, the matched-convention
CSPICE residuals are $\LunarAngleResidual$ degrees in angle and $\LunarRangeResidual$ km in range.
Both meet the $\EarthAngleBound$-degree and $\EarthRangeBound$-km acceptance bounds. The reference
also agrees on the intermediate inertial vector, body-fixed rotation, observer position and local
coordinates within their declared bounds.

Using CSPICE's native TT-to-TDB conversion instead changes the time argument by approximately
$14.66\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ and gives a range residual of about $0.8$ mm. This diagnostic exceeds the
$\EarthRangeBound$-km matched-convention range bound. The primary result retains the shared
seven-term time prescription and the original tolerance.

All \BoundaryTotalCount{} additional analytical boundary cases satisfy their criteria. The
\BoundaryLunarCount{} lunar cases have maximum angular residual $\BoundaryLunarAngleMax$ degrees;
their \BoundaryVerticalCount{} vertical cases are a subset and return the documented zero-azimuth
convention. The \BoundaryMarsCount{} Mars cases have maximum position and basis residuals
$\BoundaryMarsPositionMax$ m and $\BoundaryMarsBasisMax$, respectively. Case-level inputs and
residuals accompany the manuscript (\ref{sec:availability}).

The \StaticControlCount{} compiler controls reject incompatible physical dimensions, frames,
coordinate centers, time scales and observer-body identities before execution, with the recorded
\texttt{E0308} or \texttt{E0277} diagnostics. Complementary runtime controls accept compatible
values and distinguish mismatched observer parameters, invalid surface coordinates and degenerate
directions. Table~\ref{tab:guarantees} identifies the API and enforcement mechanism for each
control.
